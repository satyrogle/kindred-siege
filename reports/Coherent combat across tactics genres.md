# Show the rule, then change the board

Bad news first. The research cannot tell you what fight Jacob is picturing, and the one lane mock he rejected already had most of what the evidence says makes a fight readable: real sprites, lit panels, move name banners and visible block, dodge and parry answers. So the gap is probably not polish. Across auto battlers, grid tactics, turn based JRPGs, squad tactics and mass battle games, coherence comes from the same three things. There is a hard rule for who can hit whom, and the viewer can see it (Super Auto Pets' front pets, Ogre Battle 64's columns, Darkest Dungeon's ranks, Battle Network's chip footprints). There is a visible answer to who acts next and when (Fire Emblem's forecast, the new Final Fantasy Tactics turn list, Into the Breach's numbered attacks, Wildfrost's counters). And each exchange has a fixed shape (strike, counter, follow up). Excitement comes from something else: blows that change the board (Into the Breach's push, Gears Tactics' executions that refund actions, Fire Emblem Engage's Break that cancels the counter, Total War's routs, Battle Brothers' named morale states), and big moments kept rare (about 267,000 Darkest Dungeon players subscribed to one mod that doubles combat speed). Kindred Siege's locked pairs already give it the first part at Super Auto Pets strength. It lacks the second. On a 3 by 3 grid where units stand on their panels and trade, nothing changes except health, so the grid reads as decoration and the units, in Jacob's words, are "waving in the same place". My read, which is inference: his Battle Network request asks for spatial coherence (attacks have footprints, position matters), the locked pairs give relational coherence (who fights whom), and the rejected one lane mock was the relational kind at its purest. The top build direction is to give the grid stakes: footprints that flash before they land, heavy blows that push units across panels, a band that visibly breaks, and one loud blow at a time even while all five pairs fight at once. Confidence: **LIKELY** on the mechanisms, **GUESS** on what Jacob wants.

*Source labels. DEV = the studio's own staff, posts or patch notes. OFFICIAL = publisher page. PRESS = a journalist reporting or paraphrasing staff. COMMUNITY = fan wikis, guides, forums, mods. STUDY = academic research. SNIPPET = seen only in a search summary, page not opened. Inference = mine, from no source. Project record = DESIGN_LOCKS.md or HANDOFF_LOG.md in this repo.*

## Every readable genre limits who can hit whom, and shows the limit

The clearest fights give each unit one legal target, or very few. In Super Auto Pets only the two front pets fight, they hit each other at once, and the next pet steps up when one dies (COMMUNITY, [note.com write up](https://note.com/massan_gabagaba/n/n9520a392a633?hl=en)). Hearthstone Battlegrounds attacks go left to right, the boards take turns, and the target is random unless a Taunt is up (COMMUNITY, [Hearthstone wiki](https://hearthstone.fandom.com/wiki/Battlegrounds)). That random target is the rule players complain about most, and they have asked Blizzard to cut it (COMMUNITY, [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/improving-attack-order-rng-with-a-tipping-system-in-bg/155660)). Clash Royale gives every troop a target type, so a Giant or Hog Rider walks past troops to hit buildings and its job reads from its behaviour (COMMUNITY, [Clash Royale wiki](https://clashroyale.fandom.com/wiki/X-Bow)). Dota Underlords melee units prefer straight lines and take diagonals only when nothing else is left (COMMUNITY, [Underlords wiki](https://dotaunderlords.fandom.com/wiki/Attacks)). Teamfight Tactics and Mechabellum allow many legal targets, so they lean on hard counters (splash beats chaff, chaff beats single target, single target beats splash) and big spell shapes instead (COMMUNITY, [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/mechabellum-all-counters-explained/)). The pattern, which is inference: the fewer targets a unit may pick, the easier the next blow is to predict.

The squad grids make the limit spatial. **Ogre Battle 64 puts each squad on a 3 by 3 grid**, the same size as a Kindred Siege band. A unit hits its own column or the one beside it, melee must go through the first body in the column, and each class gets a fixed number of attacks by row, such as a Paladin's 3 in front, 2 in the middle and 1 at the back (COMMUNITY, [LP Archive mechanics dump](https://lparchive.org/Ogre-Battle-64/Update%2002/)). Unicorn Overlord's default, with no tactics set, is to attack the enemy directly in front (COMMUNITY, [RPG Site, from Atlus](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/15481-unicorn-overlord-highlights-tactics-priority-and-battle-stages-along-with-several-new-non-human-allies)). Darkest Dungeon's four ranks came from one picture. Chris Bourassa: "You couldn't hit somebody all the way behind three other guys with a sword" (DEV via PRESS, [GameBanshee summary of RPS interview](http://www.gamebanshee.com/kucrr)). Every skill prints which ranks it fires from and which it hits, so where a hero stands tells you what it can do (COMMUNITY, [Darkest Dungeon wiki](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat_Mechanics_(Darkest_Dungeon))). Mega Man Battle Network splits a 6 by 3 field into two 3 by 3 halves and gives every chip a fixed footprint: WideSword hits the column ahead, Cannon fires down the row at the first target, MiniBomb lands 3 panels ahead (COMMUNITY, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mega_Man_Battle_Network); [Mega Man wiki](https://megaman.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_(Battle_Chip)); [Almar's Guides](https://almarsguides.com/retro/walkthroughs/gba/games/megamanbattlenetwork6cybeastgregar/misc/lists/battlechips/)).

Two warnings come with this. **A rule the screen shows must be the rule that decides.** Ogre Battle 64 lets you pick a squad strategy such as Attack Weakest, but column reach overrides it, and players say units "don't follow" their strategy; only Attack Leader seems to work (COMMUNITY, [GameFAQs](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/198230-ogre-battle-64-person-of-lordly-caliber/79825344)). Total War's Scott Pitkethly names the same failure: from the top down camera riflemen seem to have line of sight, a small rise blocks them at soldier height, and they move in ways that look like ignoring orders (DEV, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)). That is Jacob's complaint word for word. **Exceptions break readability first.** Super Auto Pets players still cannot explain the order of some death triggers (COMMUNITY, [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1714040/discussions/0/3198118348349750330)). The rule lists that read best are the ones players can trace. Final Fantasy XII chose gambits, condition then action with the top rule winning, so players could "manage the party without micromanaging" (DEV via PRESS, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/why-i-final-fantasy-iv-i-was-key-to-i-ffxii-i-s-ai-driven-gambit-system)), and Unicorn Overlord ranks up to two conditions per skill the same way (COMMUNITY, [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/15481-unicorn-overlord-highlights-tactics-priority-and-battle-stages-along-with-several-new-non-human-allies)).

Kindred Siege already took the strongest version of this. Claude's read of the 9 Nov grid plus contact fight found about 5 fighters swinging every second on their own clocks and 22 different who hits whom pairs in 30 seconds, and Jacob locked one opponent each, trading blows, faster one first, nobody swinging twice before the other answers (project record, [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:1374](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L1374) to [1381](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L1381)). That is Super Auto Pets' front pet rule and HoMM3's one retaliation per round, spread over five lanes (inference). The lock also carries exceptions: the back row fires on its partner's swing, the Warden steps into blows aimed next to him, the Medic stays out, Focus Fire and Fall Back break pairs, and a fighter whose partner falls takes the nearest free enemy his shape reaches (project record, same lines). Each of those is where the "doing whatever they want" feeling can come back (inference). One more trap is sourced. In The Banner Saga, where turns strictly alternate one unit per side, killing weak units first hands the strong survivors more turns, so the best play is to wound and not kill; one reviewer called it "a bizarre design decision" (PRESS, [Matchstick Eyes](https://www.matchstickeyes.com/2014/02/02/the-banner-saga-turn-based-tactics-with-a-twist/)). Re pairing after a death can create the same twisted incentive, and a bot sweep can check for it (inference).

## Fights read when the watcher can predict the next blow

The genres split on turn order, and both halves now show the future on screen. Phase games (Fire Emblem, Advance Wars, Into the Breach) move one side, then the other, and need no list. Speed games interleave units on a hidden clock. In Final Fantasy Tactics each tick adds Speed to a unit's Charge Time and it acts at 100 (COMMUNITY, [Game8](https://game8.co/games/Final-Fantasy-Tactics/archives/543470)). The September 2025 remaster added a turn list showing every coming turn, including pending spells, and highlights where an action would place you (OFFICIAL and PRESS, [Nova Crystallis](https://novacrystallis.com/2025/08/final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-details-its-new-and-enhanced-features/); [RPG Site review](https://www.rpgsite.net/review/18508-final-fantasy-tactics-the-ivalice-chronicles-review)). Triangle Strategy added enemy turn order to its bar after its demo survey (OFFICIAL, [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/triangle-strategy-debut-demo-survey-results-nintendo-switch/)). Octopath Traveler's team showed each enemy's shield count, the hits left until Break, so players could "set off their own combos" (DEV via PRESS, [Siliconera on Famitsu](https://siliconera.com/?p=683405)). Child of Light puts a short Cast zone on its timeline; hit a unit inside it and its action is cancelled and it is knocked back, and bigger moves sit there longer (PRESS, [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/1570005649)). Wildfrost gives every card a visible countdown, and its developers said side by side battles with a polished counter system are what convinced them to keep going (DEV via PRESS, [MCV/Develop](https://mcvuk.com/?p=224506)).

Telegraphs carry the same job for threats. Slay the Spire's first next move system was text you had to click, which hurt streams and planning; icon intents that grow from a dagger to a scythe as damage rises are what made the game come together (secondary, an AI summary of the Ars Technica War Stories video with the developers, [readray.ai notes](https://readray.ai/notes/1046045); treat the icon thresholds as approximate). Into the Breach shows every enemy's next attack tiles, and Subset wanted "every death" to feel "like your own fault" (DEV, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-subset-games-i-into-the-breach-i-)). Justin Ma said they "would sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time", and that early damage indicators "all over the map" were fine for the team but, once handed to someone else, "we needed to make it very clear who was doing what" (DEV via PRESS, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-into-the-breach-i-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time-)). Flooding the board "with enemies and danger zones" became "needlessly complex and hard to parse" (DEV via PRESS, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/reimagining-failure-in-strategy-game-design-in-i-into-the-breach-i-)). Battle Network flashes wide chips' target panels red before they fire and lights a charging enemy's row before the lunge (COMMUNITY, single wiki, [game mechanics wiki](https://saschb2b.github.io/game-mechanics/games/mega-man-battle-network/battle-system)). One Step From Eden colours most target tiles yellow and marks some impacts with a red square and "!" (PRESS, [Nintendo World Report](https://www.nintendoworldreport.com/review/53290/one-step-from-eden-switch-review)), though a reviewer says the escape window can be "a fraction of a second" (PRESS, [Checkpoint Gaming](https://checkpointgaming.net/reviews/2020/03/one-step-from-eden-review-punishing-paradise/)). XCOM 2's Sectopod marks an area, then fires on it a round later (COMMUNITY, [Fextralife](https://xcom2.wiki.fextralife.com/Sectopod)), and players say its pod warnings are sometimes wrong, which costs trust fast (COMMUNITY, [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/268500/discussions/0/1798529872648045787)).

Perception research says why this works. People see one thing as causing another only when the reaction follows contact within about 50 ms, best at 30 to 40 ms, and the feeling fades past 100 ms (STUDY, [Frontiers 2010](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2010.00028/pdf)). Viewers cut action into events where their prediction fails (STUDY, [Zacks and others 2010](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2010.00168/pdf)). They track about four moving things, up to eight or nine when slow, fewer when fast or crowded (STUDY, [Alvarez and Franconeri 2007](https://visualcognition.psych.northwestern.edu/publications/AlvarezTracking2007.pdf); [Franconeri and others 2010](https://visualcognition.psych.northwestern.edu/publications/FranconeriPsychSci2010.pdf)). Inference: "units do whatever they want" is a prediction failure. The watcher cannot guess who swings next or at whom, so every swing is a surprise and none feels caused. Ten fighters on two small grids is past the tracking limit before anything else happens.

Here I disagree with Jacob in one sentence: the evidence leans toward sequencing blows, and his 6 Nov call threw that out. He found one blow at a time an eyesore, wanted units to "just head on fight", and the pairs lock keeps "no 1 after other" (project record, [HANDOFF_LOG.md:14409](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L14409); [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:1380](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L1380)). His way stands, and simultaneous fights can read. Total War sells many fights at once as a block against a block, with matched two body animations kept for "major moments or kills" (PRESS, SNIPPET, [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/six-new-things-we-noticed-while-playing-total-war-warhammer)). Riot's fix for TFT Set 2's "overfilled boards" where "it was often quite hard to track what was going on" was to slow spells, give them travel time and make key casters easier to track (DEV, [Riot, Galaxies learnings](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/dev-teamfight-tactics-galaxies-learnings)). Backpack Battles shows each item filling at its cooldown rate so the next shot is visible (COMMUNITY, [wiki](https://backpackbattles.wiki.gg/wiki/Cooldown)). Inference: with every pair fighting at once, prediction has to come from each pair's own rhythm (A swings, B answers, A swings) plus one loud telegraph for the big hit the leader can parry. The parry telegraph needs real time. A Dark Souls 3 analysis puts the signal plus attack at no less than 340 ms (PRESS, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/anatomy-of-an-enemy-attack-in-dark-souls-3)).

## Excitement comes from blows that change the board

The squad tactics games all shrank their action budgets to something a viewer can count, then bent the budget with one rule that creates momentum. XCOM's Jake Solomon tried the original Time Units first and found the move and action system "far more engaging" (DEV via PRESS, SNIPPET, [GameBanshee](https://gamebanshee.com/ddq)); soldiers get two actions and attacking ends the turn (PRESS, [Game Developer deep dive](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-deep-dive-into-xcom-and-xcom-2)). Gears Tactics gives 3 AP, and a melee execution of a downed enemy gives every other squad member 1 extra AP, so executions chain. Tyler Bielman called the squad wide refund "a big breakthrough" that lets you "play more aggressively" (DEV, SNIPPET, [Unreal Engine interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/gears-tactics-uses-the-gears-formula-brilliantly-to-offer-a-fresh-tactical-experience); PRESS, [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/preview/2020/03/27/gears-tactics-might-have-what-it-takes-to-compete-with-xcom)). Ogre Battle 64 ends a fight when every unit has spent its attacks, and the side that dealt more damage wins, so the fight has a visible start, middle and end (COMMUNITY, [LP Archive](https://lparchive.org/Ogre-Battle-64/Update%2002/)). Unicorn Overlord does the same with AP gems and ends when AP runs out or a side falls (COMMUNITY, [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/15481-unicorn-overlord-highlights-tactics-priority-and-battle-stages-along-with-several-new-non-human-allies)).

The exchange itself has a fixed shape in the readable games, and a matchup can break it. Fire Emblem plays attacker strike, defender counter, then a follow up if one side's Attack Speed is 4 or more higher; the weapon triangle gives +15 hit and +1 Might, and a crit triples damage (COMMUNITY, [Serenes Forest](https://serenesforest.net/the-sacred-stones/miscellaneous/calculations/)). Engage turned the triangle into Break: hit with advantage and the target cannot counter (PRESS, [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/new-fire-emblem-engage-break-mechanic-stops-counter-attacks)), and RPGFan notes the triangle now "fuels" that system rather than adding hidden percentages (PRESS, [RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/fire-emblem-engage/)). HoMM3 stacks retaliate once per round, so the second attacker on the same stack goes free (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [GameSpy archive](https://gamespy-archives.quaddicted.com/sites/www.strategyplanet.com/homm/mac/training/combat.html)). Wargroove's crits are never random. Finn Brice: each unit has a condition, Spearmen crit beside other Spearmen, Knights after running a distance, and "it makes precise unit placement really rewarding"; he compares chained crits to Puyo Puyo bursts (DEV, [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/wargroove-creator-talks-about-commanders-critical-hits-and-pitch-sessions/)). Disgaea 5 raises each later hit in a combo up to x3 at combo count 20 (COMMUNITY, [GamerGuides](https://gamerguides.com/disgaea-5-alliance-of-vengeance/guide/basics/gameplay/battle-basics)). Advance Wars fills a CO Power meter from kills and from heavy damage taken, and its director spoke of adding "waves of excitement" (PRESS, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advance_Wars)).

The strongest pattern is that position is the stake. In Into the Breach, Subset says an artillery shot's push on the tiles beside it "is usually more impactful" than its damage, and weapon interactions are "at the heart of the game" (DEV, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-subset-games-i-into-the-breach-i-)). Ogre Battle 64 crits push the target back one row if the square is free (COMMUNITY, [LP Archive](https://lparchive.org/Ogre-Battle-64/Update%2002/)). Battle Network panels crack, break, freeze and can be stolen to shrink the enemy's space (COMMUNITY, [Rockman EXE Zone](https://therockmanexezone.com/wiki/Panels_in_Mega_Man_Battle_Network)). Darkest Dungeon moves shift their own user, and enemy knockback can strand a hero where most of its kit is useless (COMMUNITY, [Highwayman](https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Highwayman); DEV via PRESS, [GameBanshee](http://www.gamebanshee.com/kucrr)). Mario + Rabbids made movement an attack: the dash hurts what it passes through, and a team jump launches off an ally (DEV, [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2017/08/feature_mario_plus_rabbids_kingdom_battles_producer_on_the_wtf_blend_of_iconic_franchises)).

The last change is the one that ends the fight. Total War's Rome 2 manual describes a banner bar that turns yellow under a morale penalty and red when the unit "is wavering and about to break and rout", and broken units leave the field (DEV, [Rome 2 encyclopedia](https://r2enc.totalwar.com/en/manual/single-player/0087_enc_page_battle_play_phase_conflict_morale)). A Warhammer III guide says leadership falls separately from health lost in the whole battle, in the last 60 seconds and in the last 4 seconds, so a burst breaks a unit (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/total-war-warhammer-3-unit-stats-guide/)). Battle Brothers replaced a hidden morale number with five named states, Confident, Steady, Wavering, Breaking and Fleeing, checked only on visible events such as an ally cut down or an enemy leader slain, so players see "how everyone is doing" at a glance (DEV, [Battle Brothers dev blog 20](https://battlebrothersgame.com/?p=883)). In Company of Heroes 2 the whole squad icon flashed yellow when suppressed, "leaving no doubt" (COMMUNITY, [coh2.org](https://coh2.org/topic/2323/visibility)). Ogre Battle 64 squads flee when their leader dies (COMMUNITY, [LP Archive](https://lparchive.org/Ogre-Battle-64/Update%2002/)), which lines up with Jacob's rules 43 leader who retreats once his band falls (project record, [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:1336](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L1336)).

Kindred Siege's grid has reach but no stakes yet. Rules 44 as built keeps every blow of our band in its lane (0 off lane blows) and makes fights about 1.8 times longer; fighters step a panel only when nothing is in reach (project record, [HANDOFF_LOG.md:14757](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L14757)). Jacob's own question to Codex the same day was how to make units fight "instead of waving in the same place" (project record, [HANDOFF_LOG.md:14733](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L14733)). Inference: once pairs lock and nobody moves, a blow can only change a health bar. Every game above that feels exciting lets a blow also change a place, a turn or a state.

## One clear signal per class, and big moments on a budget

Unit identity in these games rides on one strong channel, not many weak ones. Riot says silhouettes are "the single most important thing for champion recognition" and that the attention an effect grabs should match its gameplay weight, judged by damage, crowd control, dodgeability and impact (DEV, [Riot, Clarity in League](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/)). Clash Royale's channel is target type, Mechabellum's is counter role, Gears Tactics' Heavy hits harder on each shot while it stays still (COMMUNITY and PRESS, as cited above; [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/preview/2020/03/27/gears-tactics-might-have-what-it-takes-to-compete-with-xcom)). Into the Breach replaced weapon text with tiny looping clips of each weapon's effect. Ma: "You could type out a hundred times, 'Damages a tile and pushes adjacent tiles,' but showing that little animation of them moving is a thousand times more effective", and weapons playtesters could not read were cut (DEV via PRESS, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-into-the-breach-i-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time-)). Kindred Siege's locked class shapes, such as the Warden's Guard the cross and the Marksman's Lane shot (project record, [DESIGN_LOCKS.md:1362](../DESIGN_LOCKS.md#L1362)), are the same channel as Darkest Dungeon's rank dots and Battle Network's footprints (inference). They only carry identity if the shape shows on the panels at the moment it fires.

The animation craft has hard numbers. Fire Emblem's GBA battle clips have separate modes for attack, critical, ranged, dodge, miss and standing, and the crit is its own choreography with spins and jumps, not a faster normal; motion frames hold 2 to 7 game frames, wind ups 10 to 30, at 60 fps, with 16 colours per animation (COMMUNITY, [GBA battle animation guide](https://fe-battle-animations.neocities.org)). Warcraft 3's Footman lands his damage 0.5 s into the swing and recovers for another 0.5 s (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [Hive Workshop](https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/movement-speed-attack-speed-cast-animation.319479/)). Sakurai freezes both fighters on a hit, longer for more damage, under a hard cap, shakes the victim, starts the hurt pose at once, and caps the freeze in free for alls because a frozen pair hands a third fighter an opening (DEV via translation, [Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)). Engage's developers asked for actions "as flashy as possible", and said motions change as characters level: early a unit "will just simply dodge", later it "will counterattack while parrying" (DEV, [Nintendo Ask the Developer](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-8-fire-emblem-engage-part-2/)). Vanillaware made Unicorn Overlord's fighters larger because its battles run themselves (DEV, [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/unicorn-overlord-interview-vanillaware)). For the parry, Battle Network's counter hit is a ready template: a coloured flash on the enemy at the opening (BN4 onward), a special sound, and the enemy freezing on success (COMMUNITY, [Mega Man wiki](https://megaman.fandom.com/wiki/Emotion_Window); [mmhp.net](https://mmhp.net/EXE/Tips.html)). Paper Mario TTYD's guard window is 8 frames and its superguard 3 (COMMUNITY, [Super Mario Wiki](https://mariowiki.com/Superguard)), so a wide safe tier and a tight reward tier are proven in a party game.

The camera evidence says cut away rarely. Fire Emblem cuts every exchange to a framed side view duel, and Engage's long scenes make battles much longer (COMMUNITY, [Reddit thread](https://lr.eu.psf.lt/r/fireemblem/comments/1bkbpxo/combat_animations_in_fe_engage_are_the_spiritual/kvzfoec/?context=3)). Darkest Dungeon keeps one side view for exploring and fighting and punches in on each action (DEV via PRESS, [GameBanshee](http://www.gamebanshee.com/kucrr)). Its players answered with mods: one removes the zoom on every hit except crits, and "x2 Combat Speed" reached about **266,795 subscribers** (COMMUNITY, [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1483754805); [zoom mod thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/262060/discussions/4/618453594749180956/)). XCOM keeps a fixed camera with 90 degree steps and swoops in only for aiming, and it cut sightlines from every unit after months of work because they became a "plethora of multicolored lines" (DEV, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/xcom-enemy-unknown-interview/); DEV, [Game Developer postmortem](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/classic-postmortem-i-xcom-enemy-unknown-i-which-turns-5-today)). Total War keeps authored two body clashes "rare enough to make them feel special and epic" (DEV, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)). Darkest Dungeon's narrator gets excited only on big hits and multi kills (DEV, [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/-i-darkest-dungeon-i-designing-for-despair-and-kicking-you-when-you-re-down)). Kindred Siege has already paid this cost once: the grid plus contact mock stretched a 30.6 s sim fight to 85 s at 1x so overlapping contacts could finish (project record, [HANDOFF_LOG.md:14798](../HANDOFF_LOG.md#L14798)).

## Four Kindred Siege takes, four different missing layers

These takes are the only evidence of what Jacob wants, so they sit next to the genre findings. The last row comes from the brief for this report; it is not in the log yet and is not checked.

| Take | What it had | Jacob's verdict | What the genre evidence says it lacked (inference) |

## Conclusion

The research moves the question. Kindred Siege does not lack readable rules; after the pairs lock it has more rule clarity than TFT. It lacks consequence on the grid. Every genre that feels exciting lets a blow move something other than health: a unit across a panel, a turn order, a counter cancelled, a morale step, a squad running. Kindred Siege's grid currently gives reach and nothing else, so the most legible fight it can make is a row of five duels on the spot, and five duels on the spot is what Jacob keeps rejecting in different costumes (inference).

The second shift is about where presentation can help and where it cannot. Banners, lit panels, contact poses and answers are all sourced good practice, and the one lane mock had them. They amplify a fight; they cannot supply its shape. That shape is a sim decision, which in this repo is Claude's, and every change to it needs Jacob's yes. The directions below are ranked by how directly the evidence ties them to his complaint. None is approved.
