# Make every blow travel, land and count

All four games build battle feel from one shared kit, and each one is strongest at one or two of the six traits. Total War: Warhammer owns weight and rarity: size and a hidden mass ratio decide who gets shoved, and authored two body kills are kept rare on purpose. Hearthstone owns the single blow: one unit crosses an empty middle, both sides take damage at once, and the shake grows in steps keyed to a number the player can already see. Legion TD 2 owns readability and the build: it deletes every signal that is not information, then lets the player build, watch, and read the result as numbers. Legends of Runeterra owns the pairing and the warning: who fights whom is set before the blow, and units about to die show cracks first. The 2D cross check adds the timing none of the four publish: hold the contact pose until the health bar settles, and scale the freeze with damage under a hard cap. Kindred Siege's 6 Nov takes each dropped part of the same sentence, which is why the owner said the units "felt like they are just doing actions": a clip played, but nothing crossed to a named body, its size matched no visible number, and the board did not change. The hybrid below, all inference, stages the fight as two short rows with a clear gap, links attacker to target before the swing, sizes every blow on a three step ladder keyed to its share of the target's health, makes every hit leave a mark, spends one authored two body moment per fight on the leader's parry counter or a chief kill, and pulses each build piece on the fighter that carries it. All of it lives in presentation. The screen may stretch time between ticks, but it never adds, moves or skips one.

*Source labels. DEV = the studio's own staff, blog or patch notes. PRESS = a journalist reporting or paraphrasing staff. COMMUNITY = wikis, forums, fan guides and mods. SNIPPET = seen only in a search result summary, page not opened. Inference = mine, not from any source. Observed by eye = the coordinator's look at footage and screenshots on 6 Nov 2026, kept in its own section and never used as a source.*

## Weight sits on the impact frame and matches a number the player can see

Hearthstone's main weight tool is a screen shake in steps, keyed to the attack value of the hitter. Players report **three steps at 3, 6 and 8 attack** in normal play, with dust and debris on the top step, and say Battlegrounds raised them to **10, 20 and 30** once its stats inflated (COMMUNITY, [Blizzard forums](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/hearthstone/t/battleground-animtions-went-down-in-quality/23086); COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/HearthstoneHeroesOfWarcraft/TropesSToZ)). Blizzard never confirmed those numbers. The same forum thread is the best evidence of where weight lives. When Blizzard sped Battlegrounds up in January 2020, the poster said hero attacks had shrunk to a light tap whatever the damage and the game had lost its weight; another player said only the long animations needed trimming, not the smashes (COMMUNITY, same thread). Developers on another forum read the shake as information, because it tells the player how much damage landed (COMMUNITY, [Make Games SA](https://makegamessa.com/discussion/comment/30280/)). The shake confirms a number already on screen, so it never lies (inference). Blizzard added a shake toggle in July 2020 (COMMUNITY, [hearthstone.wiki.gg](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Options)).

Total War: Warhammer splits weight into three levers instead of one number. A hidden per entity mass decides who gets shoved, and CA tunes it in patch notes, for example moving some Bretonnian mounts from 2,200 to 2,300 (DEV via PRESS, SNIPPET, [SI](https://videogames.si.com/news/total-war-warhammer-3-patch-notes-update-3-1)). A capped damage burst lands on contact, and in WH3 a collision hit wastes its overkill, so 100 and 10,000 damage do the same to a 90 hp model (COMMUNITY, [CA forum](https://community.creative-assembly.com/total-war/total-war-warhammer/forums/10-battles/threads/5938-thoughts-on-the-new-collision-attack-paradigm?page=1)). A short charge buff follows. CA's WH2 cavalry update on **3 Nov 2021** took the charge bonus out of collision damage and raised bracing, so braced spears visibly stop horses (DEV, SNIPPET, [totalwar.com](https://totalwar.com/?p=15220); PRESS, [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/total-war-warhammer-2/cavalry-overhaul-beta)). A 2016 player guide puts knockback chance at about 10 percent times the attacker to target weight ratio, with **no knockback below a 35 percent ratio** (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1114878285)). A fan wiki adds that knockdown needs the attack clip to carry motion; a swing in place only staggers (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/%ED%86%A0%ED%83%88%20%EC%9B%8C:%20%EC%9B%8C%ED%95%B4%EB%A8%B8/%EA%B8%B0%EB%B3%B8%20%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4)). CA also caps how far one big body's weight spreads. Hotfix 5.0.5 cut the most entities one splash attack can hit from **6 to 5** (DEV via PRESS, SNIPPET, [Hard Drive](https://hard-drive.net/author/grandemil-reyes/page/92/)), and the WH3 wounded state debuffs lords and monsters as their health falls (DEV via PRESS, [GameWatcher](https://gamewatcher.com/news/total-war-warhammer-3-lords-monsters-debuff-small-unit-size-scaling)). Size does the rest. Vanillaware enlarged Unicorn Overlord's fighters in battle so fights would feel more powerful (DEV, [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/unicorn-overlord-interview-vanillaware)), and Hero's Hour gave big units bigger shadows and gave projectiles shadows too (DEV, [itch.io devlog](https://thingonitsown.itch.io/heros-hour/devlog/222862/combat-update-heros-hour-v033)).

The 2D games supply the timing the four big games never published. Sakurai's hit stop rules: attacker and victim freeze for the same time, more damage freezes longer, projectiles freeze less, and a hard cap is never passed. The victim shakes side to side on the ground, large at first and then smaller, and blends into its hurt pose over 4 frames while still frozen (DEV via translation, [Source Gaming](https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/)). He keeps the freeze shorter than he would like in four player games, because a frozen pair hands a third player a free hit (same). A community frame count of Fire Emblem on the GBA finds the **contact pose held 8 frames or more**, with the hold set by how long the HP bar takes to drop, and the ready pose held about 10 frames before a fast swing. A fan GIF with even 10 frame holds looks floaty (COMMUNITY, [Lost Worlds blog](https://lost-worlds.neocities.org/blog/2024/10/20/fire-emblem-animation/)). At 60 fps that is about **133 ms of contact and 167 ms of ready pose** (inference, arithmetic). The heavy General gets no smear on recovery because he is too heavy for one (COMMUNITY, same).

Travel is where the four games differ most. Hearthstone treats one attack as one exchange: attacker and defender deal damage at the same moment, and deaths wait until the last combat triggers finish (COMMUNITY, [hearthstone.wiki.gg](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Combat)). Legends of Runeterra pairs each attacker with one blocker and resolves the pairs one at a time, left to right (COMMUNITY, [LoL Wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/LoR:Keywords/Block)). Its keywords work as beat patterns. Quick Attack strikes first and takes nothing back if the blocker dies; Double Attack strikes before and with its blocker; Overwhelm carries the surplus past the body into the Nexus (DEV, [Riot Support](https://support.riotgames.com/legends-of-runeterra/gameplay/keywords-and-game-vocab-legends-of-runeterra)). Legion TD 2 gives each unit an acquisition range a little larger than its attack range, so melee visibly walks into contact instead of snapping (COMMUNITY, [legiontd2.wiki.gg](https://legiontd2.wiki.gg/wiki/Pathing_%26_Targeting)), and Season 6 made mesh projectiles vanish faster and fixed effects that launched from the wrong point (DEV, [v6.00](https://beta.legiontd2.com/updates/v600-season-6/)). Total War resolves ordinary melee one entity at a time, each body playing its own attack and its own reaction, which its lead programmer called snappier; true two body matched animations are kept for major moments and kills (DEV via PRESS, SNIPPET, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/six-new-things-we-noticed-while-playing-total-war-warhammer)). The same programmer names the worst readability failure: the engine blocks a shot the player can see is clear, as when a hill no taller than a bus stop blocks riflemen. He also says battle logic runs apart from display and computes future states several frames ahead (DEV, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)).

## Readable means fewer signals, and each one tells the truth

Legion TD 2's Season 6 update is almost entirely a readability pass, and its method is deletion (DEV, [v6.00](https://beta.legiontd2.com/updates/v600-season-6/)). Full health units hide their bars. Bars are cut into **1,000 HP chunks** so a tank visibly carries more segments than a fragile unit. The King and the wave 10 and wave 20 bosses get **extra wide bars**. Team colour textures came off the units because the bars already carry team. Aura range circles went because they pulled the eye from a more accurate signal. Repeated sounds of one type are **capped at 5 per player per second**. Every removal is justified by a better signal already on screen, and a bar that only appears on first damage becomes a hit signal in itself (inference).

Hearthstone keeps state on the body. Health sits on a red drop at the bottom right, white at full and red when damaged; taunt is a shield border, deathrattle a skull, and exhausted minions float zzz (COMMUNITY, [Health](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Health); [Attack](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Attack)). Its lead effects artist says effects exist to give the player information, that class identity comes from a few fixed palettes and a family of shapes (big moons for Druid, small sparkles for Mage), and that persistent effects are kept quiet so players can follow their combos (DEV, [Blizzard blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/blog/22552047)). Riot's TFT labs found newer players could not match board models to their splash art, and that fine details fade when scaled down (DEV, [TFT dev post](https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/dev/splash-art-returns-to-teamfight-tactics/)). For detailed 128 px AI sprites that is the key warning: detail is not identity (inference).

Every game that shows state change well shows it in a fixed order, and shows the loss before the removal. WH3 health bars carry ticks at 75, 50 and 25 percent, and lazy bars drain to the new value with a visible trail, so big bursts stand out without changing how damage applies (DEV, SNIPPET, [totalwar.com](https://totalwar.com/?p=16273); PRESS, [GamingOnLinux](https://gamingonlinux.com/2021/11/total-war-warhammer-iii-gets-lots-of-improvements-for-battles)). Total War morale is a visible ladder: a wavering unit drops its order and flashes its flag, then routs once leadership has stayed below zero for **2.5 seconds** (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [Gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/totalwarwarhammer/leadership-and-unit-fatigue/z38a4f)). Runeterra's patch 3.4 added a Nexus damage preview that gets louder when the damage is lethal, and **cracks on any unit facing lethal damage** (DEV patch notes via COMMUNITY, [LoL Wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/V3.4_%28Legends_of_Runeterra%29)); patch 0.9.2 made opponent discards show the card before it breaks up (PRESS, SNIPPET, [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/116943/legends-of-runeterra-patch-notes-092-slows-hecarims-charge)). In both card games the strike resolves in full before any death. Runeterra strike effects come before death effects (COMMUNITY, [LoL Wiki](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/LoR:Vocabs/Strike)), and Hearthstone deaths resolve one at a time in the order the minions entered play (COMMUNITY, [Deathrattle](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Deathrattle)). Battlegrounds tokens join the visible left to right attack queue, so a summon takes a real turn (COMMUNITY, [Battlegrounds](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds)). Legion's break point is the hardest of all: when the last fighter on a lane dies, the rest of the wave runs for the King (COMMUNITY, [King](https://legiontd2.wiki.gg/wiki/King)). Hero's Hour marks each death with a shrinking circle in the unit's colour (DEV, [itch.io devlog](https://thingonitsown.itch.io/heros-hour/devlog/222862/combat-update-heros-hour-v033)). Fire Emblem's rule joins traits 2 and 4: the body stays in the hit pose until the bar has finished falling (COMMUNITY, [Lost Worlds blog](https://lost-worlds.neocities.org/blog/2024/10/20/fire-emblem-animation/)).

## Big moments stay big because everything else is fast

Total War states the rarity rule outright. On monster against monster moments, such as a dragon trying to fly off with a Carnasaur and getting pulled down, CA's Al Bickham said they are kept rare so they "feel special and epic when they do happen" (DEV, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)). Matched two body animations are saved for major moments or kills (PRESS, SNIPPET, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/six-new-things-we-noticed-while-playing-total-war-warhammer)). Hearthstone makes each big moment unique. Legendaries get their own short music stinger, mostly World of Warcraft themes, and Big Deal cards get one off effects built to celebrate the player's moment, each meant to feel unlike the others (DEV, [Blizzard sound blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/blog/23964694/); DEV, [Blizzard effects blog](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/blog/22552047)).

Both card game studios protect rarity by making the ordinary fast. Blizzard cut each passive trigger from **0.8 s to 0.2 s**, a 75 percent cut, after testing faster triggers in Battlegrounds, where Murloc shield and poison chains each took nearly a second (DEV via PRESS, [Hearthstone Top Decks](https://www.hearthstonetopdecks.com/minion-animations-triggers-will-be-sped-up-significantly-in-regular-game-too/)). Riot's patch 0.9.1 sped up summoning, drawing, attacking and the round start, and cut input lock out as a separate job from animation time. Its design director named Draven's multi second axe chain as the kind of solo sequence no single card should create (DEV via PRESS, [Out of Games](https://outof.games/legends-of-runeterra/921-riot-discusses-the-state-of-the-legends-of-runeterra-beta-mobile-balance-card-backs-card-styles-more)). Runeterra's set pieces are champion level ups. Later ones ship as prebuilt video, because running each one live beside the game cost memory on weak devices and needed engineers for every champion (DEV, [devtrackers.gg](https://devtrackers.gg/legends-of-runeterra/p/b130da82-if-there-are-budgetary-issues-why-not-come-back-to-simple-level-up-animations-like-in-the-core-set)).

The 2D games agree. A Fire Emblem GBA critical is a whole different choreography, not a bigger number (COMMUNITY, SNIPPET, [Fire Emblem fandom](https://fireemblem.fandom.com/wiki/File:FE6_Mage_Critical.gif)). A Darkest Dungeon mod removes the hit zoom from ordinary attacks but keeps it for crits, which shows players accept a zoom when it is rare (COMMUNITY, [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/262060/discussions/4/618453594749180956/)). Sakurai treats a freeze on a boss defeat as a cousin of hit stop for marking big moments (PRESS, SNIPPET, [Kotaku Australia](https://www.kotaku.com.au/2022/08/masahiro-sakurais-new-youtube-channel-is-a-masterclass-in-making-video-games/)). Legion TD 2 is the outlier. The research found no slow motion, camera cut or freeze for its bosses or King spells; its big moments are marked only by wide bars and popups (DEV, [v6.00](https://beta.legiontd2.com/updates/v600-season-6/)).

## The build pays off only when the player sees the pick fire

In every game here the build pays off at the moment two choices meet and the player can see which one won. Total War's cavalry update was tuned for exactly that: charging braced infantry now does much lower impact damage than charging unbraced infantry, and bracing was made much stronger than a counter charge (PRESS, SNIPPET, [Bell of Lost Souls](https://belloflostsouls.net/?p=388319); PRESS, [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/total-war-warhammer-2/cavalry-overhaul-beta)). Hearthstone's Demon Hunter designer said cards that fire after the hero attacks make the class feel proactive (DEV, [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/117568/hearthstone-interview-demon-hunters-and-making-the-game-faster)). Battlegrounds turns the board left standing into the final blow, since hero damage equals the tavern tier plus the tiers of every surviving minion (COMMUNITY, [Battlegrounds](https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Battlegrounds)).

Legion TD 2 splits play into a build phase and a hands off battle, then explains the result with numbers. Its value bar turns **yellow within 20 percent** of the recommended value and red beyond it, the scoreboard shows what the enemy fielded last wave, and the MVP score rewards making the opponent leak (DEV, [v6.00](https://beta.legiontd2.com/updates/v600-season-6/)). Runeterra's champions level up from things the deck does in play, and the game pops up progress for both players, even from the deck (DEV via PRESS, [Out of Games](https://outof.games/legends-of-runeterra/921-riot-discusses-the-state-of-the-legends-of-runeterra-beta-mobile-balance-card-backs-card-styles-more); PRESS, SNIPPET, [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/legends-of-runeterra/open-beta-patch)). The 2D auto battlers add the pulse. Backpack Battles gave specific items their own activation animations and a combat log of activations players can show or hide (DEV, SNIPPET, [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/14869193)), and The Bazaar's designer has pushed passive items toward active ones (COMMUNITY, [Bazaar Builds](https://bazaar-builds.net/reynad-interview-insights-on-the-future-of-the-game/)). A passive bonus has no moment on screen; an active trigger does (inference). Vanillaware states the link plainly: Unicorn Overlord's combat runs itself so preparation stays the point, and for that reason the team paid special attention to animation (DEV, [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/unicorn-overlord-interview-vanillaware)).

### Presentation owns the clock, the sim owns the ticks

The screen already holds each blow for 0.5 s under rules 40 (project memory, not checked in code). The card games warn against holding every blow the same: Runeterra's slow beta and Battlegrounds' near one second triggers both had to be cut (inference from the sources above). So the hold should scale with the step. Light blows pass fast, heavy blows hold, crushing blows hold longest under the cap. Because the sim is deterministic on a fixed tick schedule, tick N+1 gives the same result whenever the screen asks for it, so the screen may wait between ticks for a freeze or a hold without touching balance (inference). That is different from adding pauses inside the sim, which project memory records as breaking balance. Total War's logic already runs ahead of its display (DEV, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)). Kindred Siege can do the same by stepping a scratch copy of the state a few ticks forward to learn what the next blow will do, then throwing the copy away if the leader's parry changes the outcome (inference).

One thing must never stretch: an open parry window. No freeze, shake or unrelated clip should run while the window is open, and the leader's input must never wait on another animation. That is the lock out Riot cut as its own job, apart from animation time (DEV via PRESS, [Out of Games](https://outof.games/legends-of-runeterra/921-riot-discusses-the-state-of-the-legends-of-runeterra-beta-mobile-balance-card-backs-card-styles-more)). Freeze and shake start on contact, after the window has closed (inference). Sakurai's four player problem, a frozen pair handing someone else a free hit, does not bite while crushing blows come one at a time, but a cap on total freeze per second still belongs in the presentation (inference).

### The parry sits on top of the weight ladder

Hearthstone's top shake step and Total War's showpiece moments are the same idea: the heaviest blows are few and loud. In Kindred Siege the heaviest step is also the one the player can answer, so the ladder doubles as the parry cue (inference). Fire Emblem's held ready pose, about 167 ms at 60 fps, is a natural tell, close to the 0.2 s parry window recorded for rules 26 (COMMUNITY timing; project memory for the window, not checked in code). Runeterra's respond slot sits between commit and impact, which is where the parry belongs (inference). Two Runeterra tools then tell the leader which blow is worth it: a ghost chunk on the target's bar showing what the blow will remove, and cracks if it would kill (inference from DEV patch notes via COMMUNITY). The telegraph must be honest. A community analysis of enemy intent holds that a telegraph which lies or leaves things out is worse than none (COMMUNITY, [Enemy intent telegraph](https://saschb2b.github.io/game-mechanics/concepts/enemy-intent-telegraph)), Legion TD 2 once patched a unit to be harder to fake and clearer (SNIPPET, [Steam workshop changelog](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/541930328)), and Total War's line of sight lesson applies: never let the sim block or land something the screen shows otherwise (DEV, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-i-total-war-warhammer-ii-i-to-handle-tons-of-units-and-massive-battles)). A parried blow should read like Total War's brace: no trail on the bar, no knockback on the defender, and the attacker's momentum stops dead (inference).

### Variety comes from parts, not from pair clips

Total War's two tiers answer the scripted pair problem. Ordinary melee is one body's attack plus the other body's reaction, chosen on their own; true pair clips are rare (DEV via PRESS, SNIPPET, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/six-new-things-we-noticed-while-playing-total-war-warhammer)). Kindred Siege can build every blow from parts: the attacker's own wind up, contact and recovery; a victim reaction picked by step; a display slide across the gap; a spark from the attacker's tribe shape family, the way Hearthstone gives each class its own shapes; the number; and an outcome overlay such as parried, shield shatter, crack or kill (inference). Ten fighters, three steps and a handful of outcomes give many distinct blows with no pair authored. The uneven timing of AI generated clips matters less this way, because Fire Emblem's rule puts timing in the holds rather than the frame count: most frames on 3s, smears on 1 or 2, contact on 8 or more (COMMUNITY, [Lost Worlds blog](https://lost-worlds.neocities.org/blog/2024/10/20/fire-emblem-animation/)). The presentation can retime existing frames to that pattern with no new art (inference). Darkest Dungeon's pose to pose combat suggests a few strong poses can carry a fight when the camera and sound sell the impact (PRESS, SNIPPET, [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-gothic-sensibilities-of-darkest-dungeon/1100-6424880/)).

## Where the evidence runs thin

Timing is the weakest link. No source gives frames or milliseconds for a Hearthstone or Runeterra attack motion, and Hearthstone's GDC 2015 interface talk was not readable as text ([GDC Vault](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1022036/)). The research found no source saying Total War or Legion TD 2 use hit stop or shake, so the freeze rules rest on Sakurai and on one community frame count of Fire Emblem. Hearthstone's 3, 6, 8 and 10, 20, 30 shake steps are player claims that Blizzard never confirmed. Much of the Total War evidence is SNIPPET, because totalwar.com and several fan sites refused the fetcher. Runeterra board details the researcher remembered but could not source were left out. The by eye notes come from 360p video and store screenshots. There is also a real counter argument. Legion TD 2 and Total War read well with no hit stop found, carrying weight through scale, bars and numbers instead, so Kindred Siege may get more from the bar trail, cracks and numbers than from freeze. The first test should compare one fight with freeze against the same fight without it (inference).

## Conclusion

The six traits reduce to one sentence per blow: this fighter hits that fighter, this hard, and the board is now different. Every 6 Nov failure cut one clause. Marks and snaps cut the travel. The brawl cut the named pair. Clips with hit stop kept the hardness but cut the change. The 3D route cut the read. Pair clips kept the whole sentence but could only say it one way. Read like this, the main problem was never animation quality, which is why better clips alone did not fix it (inference).

Two things follow (inference). First, most of the hybrid is overlay and timing on top of the single clips Kindred Siege already has. Only two pieces need new art, the tribe spark shapes and one authored two body clip per fight, which lowers the stakes on AI animation. Second, Kindred Siege has something none of the four games give the player mid fight: a live answer to the heaviest blow. Putting the parry at the top of the weight ladder means the player's one input lands on the fight's loudest, rarest frames. That makes the crushing step, with its held tell, ghost chunk and cracks, the piece to build first, because it serves weight, travel, state change, rarity and the parry at once.
